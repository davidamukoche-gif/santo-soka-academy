Our story

Rooted in Dagoretti. Grown in Nairobi West. Built by coaches and families who believe in the game.

Who we are

A club, an academy, a community.

Santo Soka Academy is the youth development arm of Dagoretti Green Santos FC — a Kenyan football club based in the Dagoretti area of Nairobi. The club has a rich history, having competed in the Kenyan National Super League in 2008, and has since continued to build its name through vibrant youth programmes and a deep involvement with the local community.

Today the club plays in the FKF Nairobi West County League, while the academy — Santo Soka — trains young players from age 4 up to 20. The academy focuses on nurturing talent, discipline and teamwork, providing a platform for boys and girls to develop their skills and pursue their aspirations in football.

Under the guidance of our technical staff, the academy has seen success in local tournaments and continues to play a pivotal role in the community.

FKF Nairobi West

Grassroots football at its best.

Our senior team competes in the FKF Nairobi West County League, a competition committed to elevating grassroots football in the capital.

The FKF Nairobi West branch runs a County League designed to give young players a platform to shine and gain valuable competitive experience. Each season features a dynamic mix of returning powerhouses and new clubs, with standout performances across tightly contested derbies.

Santo Soka is proud to be part of that ecosystem. Our academy sides feed directly into the Dagoretti Green Santos senior team, giving every young player a genuine reason to believe the pathway is real.
