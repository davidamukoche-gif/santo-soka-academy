# Leo & Friends — Episode 01 Production Package

## Episode specification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Episode | EP01 — *Leo Learns to Share* |
| Category | Life lesson |
| Target age | 3–6 |
| Lesson | Sharing makes playtime better for everyone. |
| Location | Sunshine Park |
| Duration | 2–4 minutes |
| Core formula | Story + character + lesson + repetition + music |

## Character continuity

**Leo** is a curious young lion with golden-orange fur, rounded ears, expressive brown eyes, a small friendly mane, a bright blue T-shirt and a small yellow backpack. Preserve his clothing, proportions, facial features and preschool 3D animation style in every scene.

**Koko** is a playful panda who creates the funny moments and helps Leo discover the solution. **Mia** is a curious rabbit who asks the question that makes Leo pause. **Toto** is a calm turtle who reinforces the lesson at the end.

## Episode script

### Scene 1 — The toy box surprise

**Location:** Sunshine Park, sunny morning.  
**Action:** Leo discovers a bright red toy car inside a colorful toy box.  
**Facial expressions:** Leo’s eyes widen; he smiles with delight.  
**Narrator:** “One sunny morning, Leo found something special.”  
**Leo:** “Wow! A red race car!”  
**Sound:** Sparkle, soft birds, happy daytime theme.

### Scene 2 — A tricky little question

**Action:** Mia and Koko run over to see the new car.  
**Mia:** “Can we play too?”  
**Leo:** “Umm… I want to play first.”  
**Narrator:** “Leo loved the car. He loved it so much that he forgot about his friends.”  
**Sound:** Curious music cue.

### Scene 3 — Leo tries to keep it all

**Action:** Leo races the car around a small track by himself. Koko tries to follow, but the car zooms away.  
**Koko:** “Beep beep! Make room for a panda!”  
**Leo:** “My car! My track!”  
**Narrator:** “Leo had the whole track… but the game felt a little quiet.”  
**Sound:** Playful engine, gentle comic boing.

### Scene 4 — Koko has an idea

**Action:** Koko brings a second toy car made from a leaf and a stick. It falls apart immediately.  
**Koko:** “Look! My leaf car is very fast!”  
**Action:** The leaf car flops over. Everyone giggles, including Leo.  
**Leo:** “That was funny!”  
**Sound:** Soft laughter, comedy sting.

### Scene 5 — A sharing song

**Action:** Mia points to the empty track.  
**Mia:** “What if we take turns?”  
**Narrator:** “Leo thought about it. One car. Three friends. Could they make room?”  
**Song:** “You go, I go, we all get a turn. Sharing is a way that friends can learn!”  
**Sound:** Light learning theme, hand claps kept below dialogue.

### Scene 6 — The happy hand-off

**Action:** Leo rolls the red car toward Mia.  
**Leo:** “Here, Mia. You can go first.”  
**Mia:** “Thank you, Leo!”  
**Action:** Mia rolls the car to Koko. Koko makes a silly racing face.  
**Koko:** “Panda power!”  
**Sound:** Wheel roll, happy chime.

### Scene 7 — Friends play together

**Action:** Leo, Mia and Koko take turns around the track. Toto watches and nods calmly.  
**Toto:** “Everyone gets a turn. Everyone gets to smile.”  
**Leo:** “Sharing made the game more fun!”  
**Sound:** Bright friendship theme, gentle claps.

### Scene 8 — Repeat the lesson

**Action:** The friends sit together beside the toy box. Leo places the red car in the middle.  
**Leo:** “Sharing makes playtime better for everyone!”  
**Mia:** “Your turn, my turn, our turn!”  
**Narrator:** “And that is how Leo learned to share.”  
**Sound:** Ending theme, sparkle, birds.

## Music and sound

Use the happy daytime theme for the opening, a short comedy cue for the leaf car gag, the learning theme beneath the sharing song, and the reusable ending theme in Scene 8. Keep music quiet enough for every line of dialogue to remain clear. Use birds, sparkle, wheel rolls, laughter, a soft boing and gentle hand claps as the main sound effects.
